Frequently asked questions

Getting started

Q. How do I open Slip?

Press your chosen trigger key (Command by default) twice, quickly. The clipboard overlay slides in from the edge of your screen.
Slip does not appear in the Dock — use the menu bar icon for settings and to quit.

Q. Why does Slip ask for Accessibility permission?

Slip uses macOS Accessibility permission for two things:

  1. Detecting your trigger key (so the double-press works in any app)
  2. Performing the paste (so it can paste into the app you're using)

That's all it's used for. Slip never records what you type, and never sends anything outside your Mac.
Grant it in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility, and turn on the switch for Slip.

Q. I granted permission, but pressing the key twice does nothing.

Try these in order:

  1. Quit and reopen Slip (menu bar icon → Quit, then launch it again)
  2. Check that Slip's switch is actually on in the Accessibility list
  3. If an old Slip entry is stuck in the list, remove it with "−", then relaunch Slip and grant permission again
  4. Your double-press may be too fast or too slow. Adjust the timing in Settings → "Trigger shortcut"

If none of this helps, please contact us at our contact form.

Using Slip

Q. How do I paste something?

With the overlay open, any of these will paste:

  • Number keys (1, 2, 3…) — pastes that card immediately
  • Arrow keys to select, then Enter
  • Click a card
  • Drag and drop a card into any app

Q. Can I paste several items at once?

Yes. Use Shift + number keys to pick items that aren't next to each other, Command + Shift + arrow keys
to select a range, or select several with the mouse — then press Enter (or drag and drop).
They're pasted in the order you selected them.

Q. Does Slip keep images and files too?

Yes. Along with text, images and files such as videos and PDFs are kept in their original form —
files stay files, images stay images.

Q. Can I save snippets so they don't disappear?

Yes. Right-click a card → "Save to category", or press Command + S to choose where to save it.
Saved items are never pushed out by new history.

Q. Can I change where the overlay appears?

In Settings → "Position", you can have it appear from the top or bottom of your screen (horizontal bar).
Left and right (vertical column) is a PRO feature.

Q. How do I switch the language to Japanese?

Settings → "Display language" → choose 日本語, then restart Slip
(menu bar icon → Quit, then launch again). On macOS, language changes take effect after a restart.

Privacy & safety

Q. Is my clipboard data sent anywhere?

No. Everything you copy is stored only on your Mac.
Slip never sends your clipboard contents to any external server.

The only time Slip connects to the internet is when you open and submit the contact form yourself.
See the privacy policy for details.

Q. What about passwords I copy?

They aren't saved. When a password manager (such as 1Password) marks a copy as Concealed to macOS,
Slip automatically excludes it from your history.
The same applies to copies marked Transient or AutoGenerated by an app.
(Slip follows the industry-standard markers from nspasteboard.org.)

Q. How do I clear my history?

You can select and delete individual cards, or delete a whole category.
(Deletion can't be undone, so please confirm in the dialog.)
